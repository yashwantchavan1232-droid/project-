Technical Indicator Masterclass & Methodology

Comprehensive reference guide to the 50+ quantitative indicators powering INSYS PRO v4.0

Trend Indicators (12)

Macro & Directional
  • SMA (20, 50, 200): Moving averages tracking dynamic short, medium, and long-term institutional support levels.
  • EMA (12, 26, 50): Exponential moving averages giving greater weight to recent price discovery.
  • Ichimoku Cloud: Comprehensive equilibrium system utilizing Tenkan-sen, Kijun-sen, Senkou Span A & B.
  • Supertrend & Parabolic SAR: Trailing volatility stop mechanisms identifying regime breaks.
  • ADX (+DI / -DI): Quantifies directional trend intensity on a 0–100 scale.

Momentum Oscillators (10)

Velocity & Overbought/Oversold
  • RSI (Relative Strength Index): Measures velocity of price moves. Readings < 30 flag oversold bottoms; > 70 flag overbought tops.
  • MACD (12, 26, 9): Moving Average Convergence Divergence line, signal line, and histogram momentum spreads.
  • Stochastic Oscillator (%K, %D): Compares closing price against price range over 14 periods.
  • Money Flow Index (MFI): Volume-weighted momentum indicator identifying buying pressure.
  • Williams %R & CCI: Commodity Channel Index and extreme range oscillators.

Volatility & Bands (9)

Standard Deviation & Squeeze
  • Bollinger Bands (20, 2): Upper, Middle, and Lower bands measuring 2 standard deviations.
  • Bollinger %B & Bandwidth: Quantifies price position within bands and identifies explosive volatility squeezes.
  • ATR (Average True Range): Measures absolute volatility in currency units for stop sizing.
  • Keltner & Donchian Channels: Channel boundaries based on exponential averages and multi-period extremes.
  • Historical Volatility (30d): Annualized log return variance.

Volume & Flow (9)

Institutional Footprints
  • VWAP (Volume Weighted Avg Price): The true institutional transaction benchmark.
  • OBV (On-Balance Volume): Cumulative volume flow preceding price direction.
  • Volume Spike Detection: Triggers when current volume surges ≥ 40% above 20-day SMA.
  • Chaikin Money Flow (CMF): Quantifies accumulation/distribution pressure over 20 bars.
  • Ease of Movement (EOM) & ADL: Relationship between volume and price displacement.

Advanced Levels & Fibonacci (10)

Mathematical Harmonics
  • Fibonacci Retracement (61.8%, 50%, 38.2%): Golden ratio support & resistance zones calculated from swing extremes.
  • Classic & Fibonacci Pivot Points: PP, R1–R3, S1–S3 intraday predictive boundaries.
  • Sharpe Ratio & Beta: Risk-adjusted performance relative to Indian 10-year G-Sec yield and market benchmark.
  • Max Drawdown (MDD): Peak-to-trough capital decline over 250 trading sessions.
